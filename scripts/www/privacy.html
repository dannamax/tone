隐私政策

SeekerHub（以下简称"我们"） 重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们如何收集、使用与保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

为提供任务发布与领取服务，我们收集：邮箱地址（用于登录与验证码）、设备标识、发布/领取任务相关的位置信息与任务图片。支付相关交易由 Apple 处理，我们不存储您的支付卡信息。

2. 信息的使用

您的信息仅用于：账号认证、任务匹配与展示、交易对账、安全风控与必要的服务通知。我们不会将您的个人信息出售给第三方。

3. 信息的存储与安全

数据存储于香港（Hong Kong）的云服务器，采用传输层加密（TLS）与访问控制措施保护您的数据。在您账号存续期间，我们保留必要信息以提供服务；账号注销后，我们将在 30 天内删除或匿名化您的个人数据，法律法规要求更长期保留的除外。

4. Cookie 与本地存储

本 App 使用本地存储（如 UserDefaults）保存登录态等必要偏好，不用于跨站广告追踪。我们不会在未经您同意的情况下将此类标识用于第三方广告跟踪。

5. 您的权利

您可以随时在 App 内注销账号或联系我们删除个人数据。我们会在合理时间内响应您的请求。

6. 联系我们

隐私相关问题请联系：privacy@gotseeker.com

Privacy Policy

SeekerHub respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

To provide task publishing and claiming services, we collect: email address (for login and verification codes), device identifier, location information and task photos related to publishing/claiming tasks. Payment transactions are processed by Apple; we do not store your card information.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used solely for: account authentication, task matching and display, transaction reconciliation, security risk control, and necessary service notifications. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Storage & Security

Data is stored on cloud servers in Hong Kong, protected with TLS encryption and access controls. We retain necessary information while your account is active; within 30 days of account deletion we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where law requires longer retention.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

The App uses local storage (e.g., UserDefaults) to keep essential preferences such as login state. We do not use such identifiers for third-party ad tracking without your consent.

5. Your Rights

You may delete your account or request data deletion at any time within the App. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Contact

For privacy matters, contact: privacy@gotseeker.com
